# Medicare Annual Enrollment Comparison Platform

## Overview

This application was built to solve a real operational problem during **Medicare Annual Enrollment (AEP)**, which runs annually from **October 15 – December 7**.

During this 8-week window, millions of Medicare beneficiaries review, change, or update their insurance plans. The Medicare system can be complex and difficult to navigate, especially for seniors who are unfamiliar with coverage structures, plan comparisons, and county-based plan availability.

This platform provides a streamlined, self-service solution that allows users to:

- Locate their current Medicare plan
- Compare available plans in their county
- Evaluate plan differences clearly and efficiently
- Request contact or schedule meetings directly through the site

---

## Problem

Before transitioning into software development, I worked as an insurance broker at my father's brokerage.

Each AEP season, our firm must contact over **1,500 clients** to confirm whether they want to:

- Remain on their current plan
- Review alternative plan options
- Schedule a consultation

With only **3 brokers**, it is not operationally feasible to personally connect with every client during the 2-month enrollment window.

This created a scalability problem:
- High client volume
- Limited time
- Limited broker capacity
- Risk of missed opportunities

---

## Solution

I developed a web application that allowed clients to independently:

1. Search for and view their current Medicare plan
2. Compare available plans within their county
3. Submit contact requests
4. RSVP to meetings directly through the platform

The goal was to:

- Reduce broker workload
- Empower clients with accessible plan data
- Capture inbound interest efficiently
- Increase operational scalability during AEP

---

## Results

During the 2-month AEP period, the platform produced measurable impact:

- **1,400+ active users**
- **100+ direct contact requests**
- **50+ meeting RSVPs**
- **$29,000+ in new revenue generated**
- Contributed to new client acquisition

The application successfully:
- Increased client engagement
- Reduced manual outreach requirements
- Created a scalable system for seasonal high-volume demand
- Demonstrated clear ROI within its first deployment cycle
